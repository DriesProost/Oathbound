# The Eight Great Systems

This expansion uses the existing campaign's reward receipts. It adds no currency, reward claim, streak, inactivity penalty or combat system. Daily Ventures retain all 60 authored definitions, their split XP, replacement rules and historical snapshots.

## One permanent world ledger

`worldProgression.ts` is the single reconciliation boundary used by the app after a saved real-world action and during quiet date/load refreshes. It derives Keep development, welcomes the Retinue, recognises Relics, makes roadside stories available, closes eligible Chapters, derives Titles, restores world places and finally writes closed Chronicle weeks. All permanent discoveries use one idempotent append-only milestone ledger. New rewards remain exclusively in ordinary deed, Oath, Venture and commission receipts. World reconciliation cannot alter those receipts, Journey distance or weight data.

Keep levels and lifetime development come from `totals`. Earned objects remain even when an Oath receipt is corrected or the player is absent. `WorldCondition` supports nested all/any conditions for attribute levels, rank, actual distance, completed Ventures (including secondary attributes), training, confirmed kept Oaths, actual recorded days, campaign age, established Keep stages, completed Chapters, resolved encounters and earned Relics. Conditions and narrative content live outside React.

## The eight systems

1. **Retinue:** Ser Oswin (Master-at-Arms), Ysabel Fen (Scout), Brother Aldren (Scholar), Mistress Elin (Physician), Edric Vale (Chamberlain), Captain Hester (Castellan) and Tomas Reed (Bard). Each has a stable identity, biography, personality, dialogue, arrival scene, condition, location, milestone reactions and optional Venture introduction. Attribute-based arrivals have rank and campaign/Venture alternatives, so a disabled ordinary goal does not block the household. Locked rooms describe a future guest without exposing numerical requirements. Dialogue is deterministic; a remembered roadside meeting and new milestones influence remarks without permanently replacing ordinary conversation.
2. **Evolving Keep:** six areas, five stages each. Attribute levels 1/4/7/10/14 begin stages 0/1/2/3/4. Growth is recorded automatically and cannot regress. Residents are associated with the rooms, and the visual renderer can replace its current architectural silhouettes with stage images through presentation metadata.
3. **Relics:** twelve authored objects, each with an achievement condition, origin, category, room, recognition date and provenance. The Cabinet renders stored snapshots, so later content edits do not alter their origin. No random drops, reward points or trivial app-visit achievements.
4. **Illuminated Chronicle:** completed calendar weeks containing actual recorded activity or world events become fixed manuscript pages. Exact reward receipts include commission bonuses. Highlights cover distance, training, kept Oaths, Ventures, level changes, ranks, landmarks, Relics, room growth, arrivals, Titles and restoration. Quiet records are neutral; unvisited empty weeks are not fabricated. Prior factual weeks are marked reconstructed. Pages never change after later corrections/config edits. Bard stanzas are short authored pairs of lines, used infrequently when he was present and meaningful events occurred. The shared page model supports week, chapter and future monthly pages; monthly generation is intentionally not implemented.
5. **Chapters:** Chapter I, The Road to Oakhaven, uses the existing 0–30 km Journey and its four landmarks. It has an opening, milestone hooks, encounter pool, closing condition and scene, a chapter manuscript page, the brass-key Relic and a Songbook theme. It closes once, without a deadline. Future Chapters can declare their predecessor, route, conditions and content without component changes.
6. **Encounters:** ten authored Oakhaven stories: washed-out bridge, carter, monastery, scholar, merchant, old shelter, rain, shepherd, messenger and beacon keeper. Every story offers two equally valid flavour choices. Events become quietly available when actual recorded distance reaches their interval; large patrols retain any story they pass rather than losing it. Choice labels, outcomes and memories are snapshotted, recorded once and read offline. A scholar encounter is recognised in Aldren's later dialogue. Choices never award XP or Renown. Initial encounters are non-repeatable.
7. **Titles:** ten authored names based on paired attributes, rank and occasional additional accomplishments. Central defaults require level 6 in both attributes and Man-at-Arms, with stronger individual requirements where appropriate. The player chooses only among earned names; preference persists on Knight and Keep. Titles do not revoke or award rewards.
8. **Grey Host:** five permanently restored places: Eastern Beacon, Mill Crossing, Wayfarer's Watch, Oakhaven Gate and Northern Watchfire. Real road progression, Chapter completion, established rooms, rank and Relics offer meaningful restoration paths. Grey mist marks fictional unreclaimed places, never the player's health or setbacks. Lit places remain lit. There are no enemy health bars, damage, loot or lost progress.

## Music and Songbook

The existing bundled ambience and its preferences remain unchanged. No new audio is invented and no world unlock calls the audio player. Soundtrack metadata has rank, place, Keep-stage, Retinue and Chapter associations. Five rank arrangements cover sparse plucked strings through restrained ceremonial chamber-folk. Their audio remains unavailable until real tracks are supplied. The original Bard's Tale is the only playable existing piece; Chapter I unlocks the name of its closing air. With the Bard present, players can create and edit a title, lyrics/notes and chord text, with preserved creation/update dates. There is no recording upload or capture feature.

## Save v5

The current key is `oathbound.knight.v5`. A clean v4 → v5 migration validates the complete old save, retains every existing field/value and adds an empty world ledger. It does not fabricate historical arrivals, choices, Chapters or restorations. Before committing v5 it saves exact v4 bytes at `oathbound.knight.v4.world.backup`, leaves the v4 source intact, and never overwrites an existing backup. Backup failure aborts the write. Corrupt newer saves never fall back to an older source. Existing v1/v2/v3 migration and backup paths remain supported.

On the first quiet reconciliation, previously met world conditions may be recognised with today's discovery date and `discovered` provenance. The UI explicitly says recognised/recorded/welcomed on that date, rather than inventing an original date. Actual newly recorded actions use `earned` provenance. Discovery/load operations do not emit ceremonies.

Validation checks collection structure, snapshot lengths, dates, provenance, identity uniqueness, Keep stages, Relic origins, song lengths/dates, chapter-page and milestone links, encounter ranges/choices/resolution memories, and preferred earned Titles. Weekly and Chapter pages preserve their own prose and highlights. Weight and progression histories are independent of all of these collections.

## Feedback and presentation

The current five primary navigation areas remain. Keep owns rooms, Retinue, Songbook and Cabinet; Journey owns Chapters, optional encounters and restoration; Knight owns Titles; Chronicle owns manuscript pages. All are ordinary panels/expandable areas, not a new permanent navigation bar.

One existing feedback surface coordinates consequences in this order: rank, Chapter, landmark/restoration/commission, Keep/Retinue, attribute level, Relic/Title, Venture, ordinary Oath/deed, reward receipt. Reward values are unchanged by the world system. Navigation dismisses an active ceremony; reload has no action transition to replay. Roadside story availability uses a quiet navigation indicator and screen-reader status instead of an interrupting modal.

## Verification and intentional limits

All previous tests remain in the suite, with schema assertions/legacy fixtures adjusted for v5. New tests cover the eight domain systems and populated-save integration, including permanence, exact migration preservation, deterministic generation, snapshot stability, choice validation, reward isolation, no fabrication and ceremony replay prevention. Final suite: 208 passing tests.

Production build: `npm run build -- --base=/Oathbound/`. Browser check: `scripts/eight-systems-smoke.cjs` against Vite on 5173 and production preview on 4173 with `/Oathbound/` base. It covers populated v4 migration/backup, all six rooms and seven residents, Songbook editing without audio autoplay, remembered encounter choice, coordinated earned feedback, preference reload, weekly rollover, 320/390/768/1440 px layouts, landscape input focus and actual offline service-worker startup. The existing `daily-ventures-smoke.cjs` also passes against this build, including offline lesson completion and multi-attribute rewards.

Portraits/Relic icons, Keep silhouettes and frontier markers are tasteful placeholders. New soundtrack pieces are metadata only. Only Chapter I and ten non-repeatable encounters are authored. Monthly summaries, future Chapters/encounters, music recording, new music files, heraldry changes, combat and Stage 2D are intentionally outside this implementation.
