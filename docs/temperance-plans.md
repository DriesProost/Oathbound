# Temperance plans

Campaign settings offer two plans under Alcohol / temperance:

- Remain alcohol-free: the original daily Oath.
- Drink less: alcohol-free Monday–Friday, with weekends optional.

The weekday plan shows confirmed alcohol-free days out of elapsed campaign weekdays in the current Monday–Sunday week. Unlogged weekdays remain unknown. Weekends do not enter this summary. A voluntary weekend Oath must first be sworn; its confirmation earns the usual reward once.

Each dated Oath retains its existing reward receipt and correction rules. Recording a weekday drink grants no reward and removes no unrelated progress. Calendar sobriety statistics remain factual; the weekday summary is separate from consecutive-day streaks.

The optional `schedule` field on an Oath campaign target is `daily` or `weekdays`. An absent field means the existing daily abstinence plan. Campaign revisions preserve earlier plans; no save version change or historical reward rewrite is necessary.
