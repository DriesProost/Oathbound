Wisdom level 3 adds a scholar's satchel; Endurance level 3 adds a rolled travelling cloak. These are presentation details, not inventory items or new rewards. Keep and Knight share the same derived appearance; onboarding previews a squire. SVG gradient IDs are unique across rendered illustrations.

The existing 0 / 9 / 21 / 30 km route now tells an original connected story in four three-paragraph chapters. The Keep chapter is available at departure. Other chapters appear only once the actual accumulated distance reaches their landmark. Reached chapters remain rereadable from Journey. A teaser hints at the next setting without revealing its chapter. Landmark feedback retains a brief inscription, keeping action receipts concise.

No save changes, extra milestones, progression adjustments or encounters are introduced. Reopening a reached chapter creates no achievement ceremony.
