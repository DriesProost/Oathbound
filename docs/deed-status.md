# Optional deed status

An unfinished notice offers Not completed, Not planned, or Leave unlogged under Other options. Not completed follows the deed's confirmation timing; Not planned can be recorded earlier. Completing a deed later replaces its non-completion note and earns the normal daily reward once. Completed activities cannot be downgraded through these controls.

Non-completion notes are saved separately in optional `deedNotes` on save v3, with local date, quest identity, name snapshot and status. Existing saves without notes remain valid and unchanged. No negative activity, reward receipt, weekly training count, penalty or achievement ceremony is created. Chronicle includes dated notes; unlogged days remain unknown. There is no daily completion-percentage denominator to inflate with rest days.

Oaths retain their separate confirmation/correction system. Weight records remain independent.
